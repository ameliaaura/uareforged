Not part of the Quest app:
  * FFmpeg: video playback is not built for the headset (the desktop builds carry FFmpeg and its LGPL notice).
  * No Meta (Oculus) SDK component is included. The app talks to the headset through the standard OpenXR API
    (Khronos loader above); the com.oculus.* lines in its manifest are feature and permission names that the
    headset's system reads, not code.
  * No game data: Urban Assault (1998) is Microsoft's. The app downloads the community OpenUA Standalone Package
    from the Metropolis Dawn community's own server onto the headset when the player asks for it, or the player
    copies their own game files. The Reforge pack has its own licence (reforge-pack-docs/LICENSE.txt on the
    headset after the download).
